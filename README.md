# GabrielKlyatis.github.io

I am a Robotics MSE student at the [GRASP Laboratory](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/). Prior to graduate school, I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from [Tel Aviv University](https://english.tau.ac.il/) and worked as a software engineer at [Magic Leap](https://www.magicleap.com/), where I focused on embedded systems and low-level software development for augmented reality devices.

My research interests broadly lie at the intersection of robotics, computer vision, and machine learning, particularly in developing intelligent perception and learning systems for autonomous robots. I am especially interested in multimodal and bio-inspired sensing, including the integration of visual and acoustic perception to enable robots to better understand, navigate, and interact with complex real-world environments.
