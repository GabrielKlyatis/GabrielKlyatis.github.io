# GabrielKlyatis.github.io

I am a Robotics MSE student in the [GRASP Laboratory](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) at the University of Pennsylvania. Prior to graduate school, I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Tel Aviv University, and worked as an embedded software engineer at [Magic Leap](https://www.magicleap.com/).

My research interests lie in developing perception learning systems for autonomous robots. I am especially interested in multimodal and bio-inspired sensing, including the integration of visual and acoustic perception to enable robots to better understand, navigate, and interact with complex real-world environments.
