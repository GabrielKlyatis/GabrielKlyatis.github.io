Hi! I'm Gabriel Klyatis.

I am a Robotics MSE student at the GRASP Laboratory at the University of Pennsylvania. Prior to graduate school, I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Tel Aviv University and worked as an embedded software engineer at Magic Leap.

My research interests lie in developing perception learning systems for autonomous robots. I am especially interested in multimodal and bio-inspired sensing, including the integration of visual and acoustic perception to enable robots to better understand, navigate, and interact with complex real-world environments.
